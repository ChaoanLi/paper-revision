Associate Editor

The paper proposes a permutation scan test for a change in variance in short time series. The
idea is simple and potentially useful, but the paper needs a clearer statement of its
contribution and a broader simulation study.

**AE-1** 1. The contribution relative to existing CUSUM-of-squares tests is not clear. Please
state precisely what is new in the test and in the theory, and what is taken from earlier work.

**AE-2** 2. The simulation study uses only Gaussian errors. Since the motivating data are daily
returns, heavy-tailed errors should be considered.
