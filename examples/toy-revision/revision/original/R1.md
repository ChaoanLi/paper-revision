Reviewer 1

The authors propose a scan statistic over windows of length $m$ with a permutation null. The
paper is well written. I have the following comments.

**R1-1** 1. Theorem 1 requires $m/n \to 0$. How should $m$ be chosen in practice? The paper
uses $m = 20$ throughout without justification.

**R1-2** 2. The power comparison in Table 2 seems unfair: the competing CUSUM-of-squares test
is run with its default settings, while the proposed test uses the window that gave the best
power in a pilot study.

**R1-3** 3. Minor: on page 4, "significantly larger" is used in a non-statistical sense.
